A chat assistant that answers your questions and helps you find the right next step.

What it does

CareSolace is an AI assistant you can talk to in plain language. Describe what you need, and it will answer your questions and point you in the right direction.

How to use it

Select the chat button at the bottom right of this page, then type or speak your question. You can also attach a file. To start over, use the "Start new chat" button inside the chat window.

Good to know

CareSolace is an AI, not a person, and it can make mistakes. Please check anything important with a qualified professional.
