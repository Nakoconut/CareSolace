CareSolace is a chat assistant that listens, answers your questions and points you to the right next step. No waiting on hold, no forms to fill in first.

You can also use the chat button at the bottom right of this page.
Example conversation

Where do I start?
We’ll take it one step at a time. First, what would help most right now?

What CareSolace helps with

Ask in your own words. If CareSolace can’t help with something, it will say so and tell you where to go next.

  • Finding information

    Get clear answers to common questions without searching through pages of documents.

  • Working out next steps

    Describe your situation and get a short list of what to do and in what order.

  • Getting to a person

    When you need a human, CareSolace helps you reach the right one.

  • Sharing files and voice

    Upload a document or speak your question if typing isn’t easy.

How it works

It takes under a minute to get started.

  1. Open the chat

    Select “Start a conversation” or the chat button at the bottom right.

  2. Say what you need

    Type, speak or attach a file. Plain language is fine.

  3. Follow the guidance

    CareSolace replies with answers and next steps. Start a new chat any time.

About this assistant

CareSolace is an AI assistant, not a person. It can make mistakes, so check anything important with a qualified professional.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.
