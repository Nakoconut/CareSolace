CareSolace

Support any hour of the day.

CareSolace is an AI assistant curated for caregivers to help you find the right next step.

What it does

Ask in your own words. CareSolace will help where it can and tell you where to go when it can't.

Answers questions

Get clear answers without searching through pages of documents.

Suggests next steps

Describe your situation and get a short list of what to do.

Takes voice

Find it easier to speak? Use voice input instead.

How to use it

  1. Open the chat

    Select the chat button at the bottom right of this page.

  2. Say what you need

    Type or speak. Plain language is fine.

  3. Follow the guidance

    Use "Start new chat" inside the chat window to begin again.

Good to know

CareSolace is an AI assistant, not a person, and it can make mistakes. Please check anything important with a qualified professional.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.
